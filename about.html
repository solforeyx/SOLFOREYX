Our Story

One agency. Many digital capabilities.

Solforeyx was built for one reason: most businesses waste time and money juggling multiple freelancers and agencies for every part of their digital presence. One person for the website. Another for the ads. Another for design. Another for content. The result is chaos, delays, and a lot of miscommunication.

We solved that by combining the disciplines that matter most to modern businesses into a single, accountable team. Instead of hiring a Shopify developer, an ad manager, a designer and a copywriter separately, you work with one trusted partner who understands design, development, marketing and digital strategy from end to end.

Solforeyx works with entrepreneurs, startups and small-to-medium businesses around the world — from West Africa to Europe, North America and beyond. Every project is treated with the same standard: clean work, on time, with clear communication.

Alongside our agency work, we also operate our own online store at shop.solforeyx.com — selling laptops, phones, solar and power solutions, home appliances, gaming gear, audio equipment and content-creation tools to customers across Nigeria. Running our own store keeps us sharp: we only recommend what we already use and trust in the real world.
